# Sources and notes for classical_statements.txt

## B1 Feuerbach
- K. W. Feuerbach, *Eigenschaften einiger merkwürdigen Punkte des geradlinigen Dreiecks* (Nürnberg, 1822).
- H. S. M. Coxeter & S. L. Greitzer, *Geometry Revisited* (MAA, 1967), §5.6 "Feuerbach's theorem", pp. 117–119.
- Wikipedia, "Nine-point circle" / "Feuerbach point": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feuerbach_point
- Notes: internal tangency to incircle, external to excircles is the standard form; metric form NI = R/2 - r.
  Equilateral case: nine-point circle = incircle (sources either exclude it or regard it as trivial tangency).

## B2 Miquel
- A. Miquel, "Théorèmes sur les intersections des cercles et des sphères", *J. Math. Pures Appl.* 3 (1838), 517–522.
- R. A. Johnson, *Advanced Euclidean Geometry* (Dover, 1960), §184, p. 131.
- Wikipedia, "Miquel's theorem": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miquel%27s_theorem
- Notes: sources variously place D,E,F on the sides (segments) or on the side lines; the theorem holds on
  lines too (Miquel point may then lie outside). Points coinciding with vertices are usually excluded,
  or degenerate circles are read as lines ("circle or line" in inversive formulations).

## B3 Brahmagupta
- Brahmagupta, *Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta* (628), XII.21.
- Coxeter & Greitzer, *Geometry Revisited*, §3.2, pp. 56–60.
- Wikipedia, "Brahmagupta's formula": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmagupta%27s_formula
- Notes: stated for (convex) cyclic quadrilaterals; area unsigned. Crossed quadrilaterals excluded.

## B4 Malfatti
- G. Malfatti, "Memoria sopra un problema stereotomico", *Memorie di Matematica e di Fisica della
  Società Italiana delle Scienze* 10 (1803), 235–244.
- M. R. Stevanović, "Triangle centers associated with the Malfatti circles", *Forum Geometricorum* 3
  (2003), 83–93 (radius formula, attributed to Malfatti).
- Wikipedia, "Malfatti circles": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malfatti_circles
- Notes: r_1 is the circle tangent to the two sides through A (d = IA). The circles are mutually
  externally tangent and lie inside the triangle. Malfatti's original area-maximisation claim is false
  (Lob–Richmond 1930; Zalgaller–Los' 1994) and is NOT part of the statement. Steiner (1826) gave a
  ruler-and-compass construction; existence/uniqueness proofs are classical (e.g. via that construction).
