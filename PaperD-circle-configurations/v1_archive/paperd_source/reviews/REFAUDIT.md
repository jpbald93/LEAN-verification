## Material findings for the paper author

1. **No suspect journal volume or page range needed correction.** In particular, Miquel 3:517–522; Clifford 5:124–141; Lester 53:4–35; Sawayama 12:222–224; Thébault 45:482–483; Ayme 3:225–229; Grinberg 3:105–111; Blundon 8:615–626; Gilbert–MacDonnell 70:79–80 are corroborated.
2. **Clifford’s original title has no initial “A”.** Its bound volume is dated 1871; this audit does not establish the instalment’s precise publication day/year within the multi-year volume. The original is about the five-line/parabola-focus form and its extensions, not simply a restatement of the triangle-side pivot theorem.
3. **AFP Miquel needs its full title and authors.** Exact entry date: **11 August 2026**. It formalises the triangle-side-line pivot/concurrence theorem using complex coordinates.
4. **lean-genius is a real, merged June 2026 PR**, but its detailed stated scope is conditional incidence, not a construction/existence theorem. Its no-axiom/no-sorry claims were not independently code-audited here.
5. **Paper C already has a matching published Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23323062.** This is an actionable replacement for the placeholder.
6. The old Forum Geometricorum domain is unavailable; the three archived **original PDFs** were successfully retrieved and their journal mastheads checked. No verification rests solely on a secondary paper’s bibliography.
