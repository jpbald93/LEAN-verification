- `papere.tex`, `papere.pdf` — the paper.
- Lean sources: https://github.com/jpbald93/quadnewton (commit 48e94cf944b3dfbcca7358360a064e39b6336d98) and

- `reports/` — statement-level battery reports (before bridges, with bridges, final), bridge reports, prior-art sweep,
  fresh-clone receipts from both machines and their script, the author's hand-check sheet.
- `layer13/` — blind re-formalisation: blind text, blind Lean statements, the two Lean comparison files, reports,
  briefs, and the blind writer's complete tool-call log (machine names redacted).
- `numerics/` — numerical re-check script (60 digits, seed 20261014) and its byte-reproducible outputs.
- `reviews/` — the independent reviews and the diffs of the fixes made in response.
Machine names, IP addresses and home-directory paths are redacted throughout.
