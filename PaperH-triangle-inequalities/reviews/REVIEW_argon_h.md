2. **MINOR — L380–L382 (`paperh.tex`) — Redundancy remark on `kooi_of_quartic` overlooks an elementary algebraic proof and the sign of $R$.**
   *Problem:* Lines 380–382 state: "In `\lean{kooi_of_quartic}` a random search found no counterexample when the hypothesis $2r\le R$ is dropped (the proof uses it to get $M\ge0$), so it may be redundant". In fact, completing the square in $f(u)\le 0$ gives $(u - B)^2 - 4R(R-2r)^3 \le 0$, so $4R(R-2r)^3 \ge (u-B)^2 \ge 0$. Thus:
   - If $R > 0$ (or $R \ge 0$ with $r > 0$), $f(u) \le 0$ *algebraically implies* $2r \le R$ (since $R=0 \Rightarrow (u+r^2)^2 + r^4 \le 0$, impossible for $r>0$).
   - However, `\decl{EulerIneq.kooi_of_quartic}` (`EulerIneq/Kooi.lean:22`) has only the hypotheses `(hr : 0 < r) (hR : 2 * r ≤ R) (hq : f(u) ≤ 0)` and no separate hypothesis $0 < R$. If `hR : 2 * r ≤ R` is dropped without adding $0 < R$, then $R < 0$ makes $4R(R-2r)^3 > 0$, where $2R-r < 0$.
   *Exact fix:* Replace the sentence at L380–L382 ("In `\lean{kooi_of_quartic}` a random search found no counterexample...") with: "In `\lean{kooi_of_quartic}`, since $(u-B)^2 - f(u) = 4R(R-2r)^3$, the hypotheses $r>0$ and $f(u)\le 0$ together with $R\ge 0$ already force $2r\le R$; the lemma states $2r\le R$ explicitly to avoid a separate positivity hypothesis on $R$."
