## Verdict: MAJOR revision

The four libraries are in good shape and the paper's core is accurate: I checked every numbered theorem
against its Lean signature and found the prose faithful (one false remark about Euler's proof, one unproved
aside in van Aubel). The headline counts reproduce exactly. Gates 133/138/121/84 equal the number of public
declarations in each tree, and every public declaration is in `REQUIRED`. The battery table matches the four
final reports cell for cell. The 15 survivors and the 1 + 6 + 1 + 1 unused hypotheses are named correctly.
Line counts, receipts and digests match. The satisfiability paragraph does not present the hand
classification as certification.

The new v4 text still has several false or stale statements:

1. **Completion and degenerate cases (§4.1).** It claims that the 67 Paper-J statements are gated and
   tampered "with the gate and tampering conditions of Section 5" and are "part of the same archive". They are
   scratch files outside all four repositories. Two of its negative results are stated more strongly than
   what was proved, and one is false: for the stated example `a=b=1, c=1/4` a fourth sphere exists.
2. **Layer-13 paragraph.** It misnames the Bankoff blind statements (they are `blind_79/80`, not
   `blind_81/82`). It also says the existence pairing was equivalent in both directions, when the report
   classes it as DIFFERENCE — REAL, and nothing shows it was re-compared after the fix.
3. **Layer-10 paragraph and Limitation 4.** They describe quadrilateral bridges that do not exist (`powPt`,
   `convexHull`, `bridge_powPt_eq_power`).
4. **Arbelos tampering row.** It lists a test (`falsehyp_simple`) that is not in the suite at the final
   commit.
5. **Survivors paragraph.** It says no survivor is "in a theorem", but `twin_left_exists` is a Table-1
   theorem.
6. **Stale battery text.** Several sentences in the battery text refer to the 30 Oct / 53d0573 / 92c003b
   runs and contradict the final-commit table.

None of these needs new proofs except, optionally, item 1. All can be fixed in the text.
