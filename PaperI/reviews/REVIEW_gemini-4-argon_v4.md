# Review — Paper I v4 (AFM series standard review)

**Verdict: MAJOR REVISION (3 MAJOR, 10 MINOR).**
The machine-checked core of the four main developments (`paperi_quad` at `0c37206`, `paperi_circles` at `6c714e5`, `paperi_arbelos` at `0dddc6e`, and `paperi_inversion` at `c71abb7`) is sound: all numbered theorems in Sections 3–6 match their Lean signatures, hypotheses, and conclusions, the Layer-11 anchor gate passes on all 159 `\decl` names and 83 `File:line` anchors, the survivor and unused-hypothesis counts (15 survivors; 1 + 6 + 1 + 1 unused hypotheses) and the satisfiability numbers (35/118, 47/110, 77/115, 55/87; 0 linked by Layer 8b) match the final battery reports, and the prior-art framing in Section 5.1 respects the boundaries of `AFM/PaperJ/SCOUT.md` §5 (no forbidden claims of "beyond the plane" or "first 3D formalisation", and the refuted concurrency-point conjecture remains refuted). However, three major factual inaccuracies were introduced in the new v4 prose: (1) Section 5.1 ("Status") claims that all statements of the completion/degenerate subsection are machine-checked "with the gate and tampering conditions of Section 6" and are part of the archive, whereas those three files (`soddy_3d.lean`, `descartes_degenerate.lean`, `descartes_plane3d.lean`) are untracked scratch files with no `gate.sh` entries, no tampering tests, and 103 total declarations (not 67), and are absent from the clean `git archive` exports; (2) Section 6 ("Definition bridges (Layer 10)") falsely claims that `paperi_quad`'s bridge module relates convexity to `convexHull` and bridges `powPt` to `Sphere.power` (neither exists in `Quadrilaterals/Bridge.lean`, and `paperi_quad` has no `powPt`), and misattributes `paperi_inversion`'s internal helpers (`det3`, `dot`, `triple`, `det3g`, `det4g`) to the `arbelos` library; and (3) Section 6 ("Blind restatement from this paper (Layer 13)") misnames the Bankoff blind statements as `blind_81`, `blind_82` (they are `blind_79`, `blind_80`) and claims `blind_79` and `blind_80` are both equivalent to the library theorems in both directions, whereas `L13_BANKOFF.md` was run before `bankoff_exists` was strengthened and only proved `blind_80` in one direction (`B → R` plus `R → B_weak`). All three major issues are purely prose errors and can be fixed directly in `paperi.tex`.
