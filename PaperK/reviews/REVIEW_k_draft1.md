Inputs: `paperk.tex` (sha256 `6d1eb2df…`, identical to `AFM/PaperK/paperk.tex`), `paperk.pdf` (**14 pages** per `pdfinfo`, not 12), snapshot `repo_snapshot_62ccf87/`, `layer0/paperK_general_n.lean` (sha `9e28c5a3…`), `layer15/L15_COMPARE.md`, `SCOUT.md`, `EXTENSION.md`, `layer0/*.md`, `k0/formula_output.txt`, `LAYERS.md`, `namegate_k.py`. ## Verdict

**Major revision.** The draft's overall structure is honest and mostly backed by the evidence. Three things are confirmed: Theorems 3.4/3.6 and Morita are not formalised (there is no hyperplane, `T_i` or `R_P` anywhere in the Lean files); the general-n file really is outside the gate at `62ccf87`; and L14b is partial. Every number I could reproduce matches. That covers gate 60 = 24 + 36 counted from `gate.sh`, the line counts 272/332/217/438, the four hashes against the receipt and the snapshot, 9 tamper tests, the L15 counts (17/44/16 and 0 E / 6 O / 11 N), the name gate (54 decls / 18 anchors), and all battery figures against `REPORT_BATTERY_bb1241a.md`.

But several numbered theorem statements do not say what the Lean proves:

- In Theorem 1 the spanning hypothesis quantifies over all vectors, not cycles.
- In Theorem 4, P must *not* be assumed null. If it were, the constant would be 0.
- Theorem 6 gives the wrong vector as "the fifth cycle".
- Theorem 3 calls non-null null-space vectors "cycles".

The hand derivation linking the explicit formula to `pt(G·cofactor)` is wrong as written. I checked numerically: with the matrix named in the text the two points differ. L15's derivation is the correct one. The abstract over-claims in four places:

- the signature is (n+1,2), not (1,n+2);
- the cofactor construction exists only at n = 2 and uses n+2 rows;
- "characterisation" is claimed where only one direction is proved;
- "necessary" is claimed where there is one n = 2 counterexample.

"gated (60 declarations)" also reads as covering the general-n results. The prior-art nonexistence sentence is stronger than SCOUT.md supports, and the primary source's title in the bibliography does not match the one the scout records. All of these can be fixed in wording. None needs new Lean.
