## Checks of the edits and unchanged claims

### The mathematics at the repaired boundaries

- **Cofactors:** the square matrix has rank 3 and zero maximal-minor vector, while `(0,0,0,1,1/2)` is a finite orthogonal vector. The new separation between Laplace orthogonality and a nonzero v-slot is essential and correct. For the orthogonality-system matrix, the cofactor generates its kernel at full row rank; for the matrix of cycle rows, the B-orthogonal candidate is its **G-transformed** cofactor, as §3 explicitly states. I do not read the revised prose as removing that transform.
- **Plane sign:** for `C=(r,c,1,(r²−‖c‖²)/2)` and `H=(1,n,0,−h)`, `B(C,H)=r−n·c+h`; `B(H,H)=1−‖n‖²`. Thus the new encoding and proposed normalisation agree. For the square's three noncollinear equal-circle centres, a common oriented line would require `n·cᵢ` to be constant; their differences span R², forcing `n=0`, contrary to unit norm. This supplies the stated hand exclusion, not a Lean theorem.
- **Finite equilateral point:** the three-circle theorem follows by repeating the first circle in the fourth slot. Adding the fourth unit circle at `(0,1)` forces `P=t(1,0,0,0,1)`, hence v=0. The new existence caveat is correct.
- **Explicit point and radius:** `s19_RB` really has the corrected vector `(q₀,−q₁,−q₂,2q₃,−q₄)`, proves its orthogonality to the affine circles and v-slot `−2D`, and proves both centre and radius formulas. Its dependency is the six-statement conjunction R19, not a newly named theorem in the gated library. The abstract and §5 now make that distinction.
- **Other changed scope:** the scaled tetrahedral family scales centres as well as radii; the stated radii `s(√3+1)` and `s(1−√3)` match `ThreeD.lean:157–187`. The general-n circumradius paragraph now correctly distinguishes the coordinate squared-vertex-norm identity from a theorem mentioning Mathlib's circumradius, and `dist_sq_const` indeed permits any common radius. No new defect was found in the six numbered theorem statements.

### Concrete witnesses for the intended tamper mutations (A12)

These are independent exact calculations, **not fresh Lean certificates**, and not evidence that the harness diagnoses the cause of a build failure.

1. `falsehyp`: `X=(1,0,0,1,1/2)`, `Y=(1,0,0,1,0)`. Then `B(X,X)=0`, `B(Y,Y)=1`, and the proposed identity has LHS `−1`, RHS `0`.
2. `falsestmt`: the same null X has `r²−a²−b²=1`, whereas the mutated `3vw=3/2`.
3. `falsehyp2`: set all three centres equal to `(0,0)`, `r₀=ρ=0`, and test `(x,y)=(1,0)`. All three powers are 1, but D3=0 makes both Cramer coordinates 0 under Lean's total division; the claimed iff is false.
4. `falsestmt2`: use the four tetrahedral unit spheres, choose the fifth to repeat the first, and `P=(0,0,0,0,1,1)`. P is orthogonal to all five and has v=1. The mutated enclosing sphere has radius `√3+2`, so its B-pairing with a vertex sphere is `(3−(√3+1)²)/2=−1/2−√3`, not zero.
