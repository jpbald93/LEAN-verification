## Verdict

**Major revision. It is not depositable as it stands, but the fixes are text-only and small.** The core of the paper holds up under an independent read. Every numbered theorem matches its Lean signature. All 67 qualified `\decl` names resolve in the right namespace, and all 18 `\src` anchors land on the right line. The displayed mathematics re-derives exactly: the tangency defect, the determinant formula, the link `P=(q0,−q1,−q2,2q3,−q4)` with `P_3=−2D`, the plane encoding, and the Theorem 6 counterexample. The completion-map negative result also reproduces digit for digit from the published script. Two defects survive both earlier rounds, and both block deposit:

1. The availability section says the supporting material is published, but the paper cites three evidence files that are not in the published tree, including the record behind one of its two headline negative results.
2. §11's "What is new here" list presents mathematical statements as new: the equal-power property, which is the classical Laguerre radical centre, and the square "gap in the source". The prior-art scout searched only for formalisations, never the mathematical literature, so it does not support that framing.

The other findings are MINOR. Most are provenance or wording residues of the kind two rounds of edits leave behind.
