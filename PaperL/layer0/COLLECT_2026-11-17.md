Spec corrections that the jobs force on the plan:
1. **The literal matrix `K n` of the brief is wrong for even n** — with `j = 0…⌊n/2⌋` in both blocks it is
   `n × (n+2)` and `finrank ker ≥ 2` for every even n (`K_finrank_ker_ge_two`). The working matrix is
   `Kf n` (`n × (n+1)`, `Q`-block `j = 0…n−⌊n/2⌋−1`). PLAN wording is being amended to `Kf`.
2. **The rank hypothesis is exactly "one nonzero maximal minor"** (`P_m ≠ 0` for even n, `P₀ ≠ 0` for odd n), no
   Vandermonde or distinctness condition. `GenPos4` suffices at n = 4, `CliffordNondeg` (k3 ≠ 0) at n = 5.
3. **The kernel description needs `P₀ ≠ 0`**: when k3 = 0 the cross-vector circle form vanishes identically
   (`oddCircleEq_five_degenerate`) and the line case of `cliffordChain_two_clean` is not recovered by the kernel —
   it needs k0, which is not a kernel coordinate. So the odd-step statement must be conditional on the minor.
