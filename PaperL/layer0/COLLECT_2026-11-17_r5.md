Structure (report §Target 1–4, and I read the proof):

- **`KPhi m x y`** — the `2m+1`-line matrix whose columns are the parent's `P₁…P_{m+1}, Q₀…Q_m`
  (`KPhi_sub_eq` : `KPhi m (x∘i.succAbove) (y∘i.succAbove) = (subRows m x y i).submatrix id Fin.succ`).
  This is the correction the brief needed: **swapping `x ↔ y` does not permute the columns of `KP (2m+1)`**
  (the swap produces a `P_{m+1}`-type column that `KP (2m+1)` does not have), so the reversal lands on `KPhi`.
- **Odd reversal:** `blockRevOdd`, `sign_blockRevOdd = 1`, `prod_blockSignOdd = (−1)^(m+1)`, `KP_swap_entry_odd`,
  `crossVec_KP_odd_swap : crossVec (KP (2m+1) y x) j = blockSignOdd m j · (−1)^(m+1) · crossVec (KPhi m x y) (blockRevOdd m j)`.
- **Fact 1:** `conj_crossVec_odd_P0 : conj w₀ = (−1)^m · w₀`.
  **Fact 3:** `conj_crossVec_odd_last : conj w_last = −A′`, `A′ = crossVec (KPhi m x y) ⟨m+1⟩`
  (the maximal minor deleting `P₀` and `Q₀`; at `m = 2` this is `kA46`).
  **Fact 2:** `conj_subVec_Q0 : conj w_{m+1} = (−1)^m · C₂` (via `cramer2_Pm1` rewriting `C₂` as a `KPhi` coordinate).
- **Fact 4 / Plücker, general `m`, no Plücker algebra:**
  `plucker_odd : w₀·C₁ + (−1)^m·w_{m+1}·C₂ + w_last·A′ = 0`, proved by `cramer_three` applied to `w` re-inserted with a
  zero at `P_{m+1}` (`subRows_mulVec_ins`, `elim3`, `ins_eval`, `cramer3_P0/Q0/Qm`). At `m = 2` this is
  `k0k3_sub_k1k2`.
- **`stepEven_alg`** (field-level, `[Field K]`): from `P : w₀C₁ + s·w₁C₂ + w_l·A = 0`, the cleared relation `CL`,
  `z·v₁ = −v_l`, `z̄·v₀ = v₂`, and `v₀,v₁ ≠ 0`, conclude the `oddCircleEq` shape. `stepEven_gen` instantiates it with
  `s = (−1)^m`, `plucker_odd`, `stepEven_cleared_gen`, `conj_levelPointN_even` and `div_mul_cancel₀`.
