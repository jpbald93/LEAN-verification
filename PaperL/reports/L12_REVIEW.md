# Layer 12 review

**MAJOR — yes, the draft claims more than the supplied evidence supports.** The principal defects are an incorrect introductory description of the recursion, a narrower prose definition of generalized circle than the displayed predicate, an incorrect explanation of where the sphere extension enters, and unsupported two-machine verification receipts. The anchor tables themselves are accurately transcribed, but they do not cover many of the declarations on which the statement section relies, so their types and hypotheses cannot be independently audited here. This is not evidence that the Lean proof is false. Most of the recorded battery numbers and caveats are reproduced faithfully, and the draft correctly credits Carver rather than claiming the at-infinity classification as its own mathematics.
