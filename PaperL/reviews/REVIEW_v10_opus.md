## Verdict on the two flagged assertions

**(a) Partly supported.** The lemma's hypotheses `hv : V ≠ 0`, `hmid` and `hP0` are inconsistent by construction. The docstring says so, and the proof is `absurd (parent_crossVec_zero_of_mid k c i hmid hP0) hv` (`MorleyKernel.lean:5036–5049`). So no proof obligation reaches the conclusion, and all four surviving mutants are in the conclusion: "empty branch, conclusion mutated" is right. But "four mutants of the conjunct `u ≠ 0`" is wrong. Only two (`@18`, `@20`) touch `u ≠ 0`. `@186` flips the per-`j` finiteness antecedent and `@351` flips the final incidence equation (m1). Note too that the lemma's inconsistency goes undetected by layer 5 (`ok` on both machines) and by layer 8 (unresolved), as §5.8 already says.

**(b) Supported in substance, as a hand argument.** In `scratch/BlindCompareFull.lean`, `BlindCliffordFin = BlindCliffordP true`, and every level carries `plane = true → X ⊆ BlindPlane` with `BlindPlane = range coe`. The line branch of `BlindIsGCircle` is `insert ∞ …`, so no line is admissible, and the circle branch needs `0 < r`. At level 3 the recursion fixes each of the three points as `BlindSecond` of a pair. For the `BRIDGE_NOTES.md` §2 triple (`1+i`, `2`, `3/2+i√3/2`), I checked symbolically that the second points `2+2i`, `(1−√3/2)+i(1+√3/2)` and `3+i√3` are distinct and exactly collinear, and that the centres are pairwise transversal and not collinear. So no blind finite-plane level-3 object exists, the left side of `BlindN4` fails at `n = 3`, and the biconditional with `n ≤ 5` is false. The library's `cliffordChain_iff` is unaffected, because `Chain`/`IsGCircle` on `Set ℂ` admit lines. Two caveats: this is not machine-checked (m3), and the `BlindGenPos` membership of the triple is recorded only in prose in the notes.
